Session State After Turn 1 Stored in SessionEntry (runtime.py) — persists until Turn 2 arrives

Why session state? Turn 1 and Turn 2 are completely independent HTTP requests — once Turn 1's response is sent, the backend has no memory of what just happened. When Turn 2 arrives it looks identical to any other fresh request. pending_clarification is stored in SessionEntry so Turn 2 knows to skip routing and resume the pinned tool — without the frontend needing to send anything special. The UI just sends the user's answer as a normal message; the backend figures out the rest from its own state.
